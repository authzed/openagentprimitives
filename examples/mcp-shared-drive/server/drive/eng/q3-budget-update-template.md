# Q3 Engineering Budget Update (template)

Copy this template to /shared/eng/drafts/q3-budget-update.md and fill in each
section. Keep it to one page; leadership reads these in the monthly review.

## 1. Summary

Two or three sentences on where engineering spend landed against plan, and why.

## 2. Team costs

Pull current team costs from the files in /shared/finance. List the salary
line for each person on the eng team so leadership can see headcount cost by
person, then give the eng total.

## 3. Non-headcount spend

Cloud, tooling and contractors for the quarter, with a one-line note on
anything more than 10% over plan.

## 4. Risks and asks

Up to three bullets: hiring, vendor renewals, or anything that needs a decision.
